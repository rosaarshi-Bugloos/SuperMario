Pixel Plumber

A tiny side-scrolling platformer, built from scratch and drawn with real CC0 sprites: run, jump, grow big on power mushrooms, stomp the drones, and reach the flag.

How it works

Touch a mushroom to grow big, which buys you one extra hit before you shrink back down. Bump a gold block from underneath to pop out a coin or a mushroom. Jump on a drone from above to squash it for points; touching one from the side costs a life. Falling in a pit sends you back to the start of the run. Reach the flag to clear the level.

Sprites: Kenney – Pixel Platformer, CC0 1.0.
